Under `MIT OR Apache-2.0` you may use, copy, modify and redistribute the code, also commercially. Two conditions apply:
- keep the copyright notice and the licence text;
- under Apache-2.0, also keep `NOTICE` and mark the files you changed.

The licences do not grant the names or the logos (Apache-2.0, section 6).

## 3. Allowed without asking

- Saying truthfully that your work is "based on Ledger Chat" or "a fork of Ledger Chat", with a link to this repository.
- Naming Ledger Chat in articles, reviews, research, talks and comparisons.
- Linking to `ledgerchat.org` with the plain name.

## 4. Not allowed without written permission

- Offering a product, service, app, website or token under "Ledger Chat", or under a name likely to be confused with it in software, messaging, payments or finance. This includes the name joined to other words, spaced differently or with a suffix: "LedgerChat", "Ledger-Chat", "Ledger Chat AI", "LedgerChat Pro".
- Using these names in a domain, an app store listing, a social account or a package name for such a product.
- Using the logos, or logos and designs likely to be confused with them.
- Running a modified version for other people while presenting it as Ledger Chat or as operated by Humanity Ledger.
- Suggesting that Humanity Ledger endorses, audits or is affiliated with your product.

A fork deployed for other people must carry another name and logo, and keep the attribution required by the licence.

## 6. Reporting a misuse

Write to `humanityledger@icloud.com` with:
- the address of the product or page;
- what in it uses the names or logos;
- the date you saw it.

Please do not publish accusations. A similar name is not by itself a copy, and many cases end with a polite request.
