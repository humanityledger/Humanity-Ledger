# Sticker credits

## Hoops, Hammy and Noel (offered since 24 November 2026)

Raster illustrations supplied by the operator from `D:\Humanity-Ledger-main\public\stickers`,
`stickers2` and `stickers3`. The original file names (for example `15374836.png`, `9120678.png`,
`jugador-de-baloncesto.png`) match the naming of a stock icon library, most likely Flaticon.

**Licence: to be confirmed by the operator before public release.**

- With a Flaticon Premium (or equivalent) licence, attribution is not required. Record the licence
  holder and date here.
- With a free licence, attribution to each author is required wherever the stickers are shown, and
  the authors must be named here and in the app's credits.

What was done to the files: 40 of the 66 were chosen; metadata stripped; Hammy (256 px) and Noel
(128 px) resampled to 512 px with Lanczos, colour bleed under transparent edges and an unsharp
mask; all saved as lossless WebP. No drawing was changed.

| Pack | Stickers | Source folder | Source size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hoops | 14 | `stickers` | 512 px |
| Hammy | 16 | `stickers2` | 256 px |
| Noel | 10 | `stickers3` | 128 px |
