# Third-party marks used by the site

Estado: vigente · Fecha: 19/11/2026 · Dueño: Stefan Antonio Cirisanu · Fuente de verdad: este archivo y `BRAND_FILES` en `lib/landing/content.ts`

Each file that `BRAND_FILES` points to has one entry here, with its source, its download date and the conditions of use. `test/unit/landing/brand-files.test.ts` fails if a file in `BRAND_FILES` has no entry. Marks are shown at the size of the surrounding text, never as a seal or badge, and never with "partner", "official" or "backed by". The footer says: "Aztec and Ethereum are trademarks of their respective owners."

## /brand/ethereum/eth-diamond-black.svg

- **Source:** the `ethereum/ethereum-org-website` repository, `public/images/assets/svgs/`, commit afad4e9f (18/11/2026). These are the files behind https://ethereum.org/assets/. Details in `public/brand/ethereum/README.md`.
- **Downloaded:** 19/11/2026, unchanged.
- **Conditions:** the assets page offers the files to download and states no usage terms on the page itself (read 19/11/2026). ethereum.org links its general terms of use from the footer. **Pending (Stefan, with counsel):** read those terms and record here whether they cover this use. Until then the diamond is used only to name the network, beside the word "Ethereum".

## Aztec (not used: decision D-1)

`BRAND_FILES.aztec` is `null`, so the site writes "Aztec Network" in text, linked to https://aztec.network. The files in `public/brand/aztec/` are not shown on the site. To show the wordmark again, Stefan adds Aztec's written brand rules for this use here, with the date and who confirmed them, and sets `BRAND_FILES.aztec` in the same PR.
