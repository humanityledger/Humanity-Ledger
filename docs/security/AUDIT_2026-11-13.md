# Contracts and tokenomics review, 13 November 2026

An internal review by the engineering assistant, not an external audit. It does not replace one: the launch condition "fee contract externally audited" in `lib/protocol/tokenomics.ts` stays false. Noir and Solidity could not be compiled in the review session; every contract change below is checked by CI (Aztec contracts, Noir circuits, Contract Tests) before it counts.

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Component | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V-1 | High | TimeLockVault | `executeDeadManSwitch` swept only the tokens the caller listed, then refused every later call. Anyone could fire it with one token after the timeout and freeze all other tokens for good: the owner cannot withdraw a triggered vault and the backup could no longer be paid. | Fixed: the vault records its tokens (at most 32) and sweeps all of them; it can be called again to sweep what is left; a failing token keeps its balance instead of blocking the rest. |
| V-2 | High | TimeLockVault | Deposits credited the requested amount. With a fee on transfer token the vault held less than it credited, so the last owners of that token could not withdraw (pooled balances). | Fixed: credit is the balance difference. Test with a 10% fee mock. |
| V-3 | Medium | TimeLockVault tests | The invariant tests called a three argument `setupVault` and expected revert strings; the contract has two arguments and custom errors, so Contract Tests failed on main. | Fixed: tests follow the contract; new tests for V-1 and V-2. |
| SP-5 | Medium | LedgerSponsor | The voucher digest covers `voucher_id` and `expires_at` only, not the contract address, chain id or rollup version. The nullifier is siloed per contract, so one voucher pays once on every instance that trusts the same key (a testnet and a mainnet instance, or an old and a new mainnet instance during a rotation). | Mitigated off chain: on Alpha the signer must be its own `AZTEC_SPONSOR_SIGNER_KEY` (never derived, never shared with testnet). Contract fix pending: digest V2 = sha256(tag ‖ this_address ‖ chain_id ‖ version ‖ voucher_id ‖ expires_at), with new TXE vectors; it changes the class id, which is free while nothing is deployed. |
| SP-6 | Medium | Sponsor signer | The signer key was derived from `JWT_SECRET`: whoever can forge sessions could mint vouchers, and rotating the secret silently broke every voucher. | Fixed for Alpha (explicit key required); testnet keeps the derivation. |
| T-1 | High | Tokenomics | Buyback and burn names a 7 day TWAP "on the open market", but no HLC market exists on Aztec. A TWAP over a thin pool is cheap to move. | Open, decision needed: name the venue, a minimum liquidity before any buyback, and a maximum daily buy relative to pool depth. |
| T-2 | High | Tokenomics | The 45% airdrop is weighted by settled escrow activity. Two accounts of one person can escrow to each other and farm it; `sender != recipient` stops only the same address. | Open, decision needed: weight by fees actually paid (farming then costs at least what it earns), cap per verified identity (the identity registry), and snapshot dates announced after the fact. |
| T-3 | Medium | Tokenomics | `teamVested` releases 18/48 (37.5%) of the team allocation at month 18, because the line starts at genesis. | Open: keep (and say it plainly on /protocol) or start the line at the cliff. |
| T-4 | Medium | Tokenomics | Staking lowers agreement fees: without a minimum stake age, a payer can stake for one transaction and unstake (flexible lock). | Open: fee discounts only for stake older than the agreement, or for locked stake. |
| T-5 | Low | Tokenomics | `agreementFee` applies the minimum fee to any amount: 10 base units with a 500 unit minimum pay 500 (50 times the payment). | Open, by design today (tested): the client should refuse agreements whose fee would exceed a set share of the amount. |
| E-1 | Info | PrivateEscrow | Claim and refund share one settlement nullifier; claim must land by the deadline, refund only after it; deposits are one per (id, sender). No issue found. | No change. |
| S-1 | Info | PaymentStream | A cancel measures accrual at an anchor the sender picks, so an old anchor withholds up to 600 s of accrual from the recipient (documented as SEC-S12). Overflow-free accrual and per-state nullifiers hold. | No change; the bound is shown to users. |
| H-1 | Low | HLC token config | The wallet keeps HLC under the slot key `USDC` (the symbol shown is HLC). Nothing prices it at one dollar today; any code that treats the `USDC` slot as a stablecoin would be wrong. | Open: rename the slot when the token list next changes. |

## What has to happen before value is at risk

1. Supply locked on chain: mint the fixed supply, revoke minters and renounce the HLC admin (launch condition `supply-locked`).
2. Sponsor digest V2 (SP-5) compiled, tested and deployed with a dedicated key.
3. Decisions on T-1 and T-2 written into `lib/protocol/tokenomics.ts`, with tests.
4. An external audit of every contract that will hold funds on Alpha.
